Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product has not been activated. After activation, refunds are limited to a partial credit that depends on the plan. Annual plans receive a prorated credit for the unused months, while monthly plans are not refunded once the billing cycle has begun.

Refund requests are submitted through the billing portal and are reviewed by the payments team. The standard review time is 3 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear on the customer statement within 7 to 10 days, depending on the bank.

Exceptions exist for duplicate charges and for outages that exceed the service level agreement. In those cases the payments team issues the refund automatically and notifies the customer by email. Enterprise customers with a signed contract follow the refund terms written in that contract instead of this policy. Gift cards and promotional credits are never refundable for cash.
